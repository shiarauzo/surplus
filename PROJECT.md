## Goal

A watch app for claiming known surplus food from a nearby restaurant. The person swipes between a map of the closest restaurants and each listing. Every pickup has the same small price, paid at the counter.

## Done when

- The watch shows the map and the three nearest listings, in order of distance.
- Swiping left and right moves between those pages.
- Each listing shows the dish, the restaurant, the walk time, and the same counter price.

## Current state

The four pages are built as a watchOS app named Cerca: the map, then La olla, Menú Rosa, and Don Pan. The counter price on screen is S/ 8.50.

## Next action

Run Cerca in the watchOS simulator and check the swipe.
